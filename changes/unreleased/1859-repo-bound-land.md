Landing is bound to the repo `--project` selects (team §9.5). `mdstack train` records the selected project's `context` in its train and delegation records and, before any clone, branch, record, push, CI dispatch, merge or cleanup, reads every record and delegation an action names and the predecessor train each builds on, every member qualifier (`<owner>/<name>#<n>`) and the URL of every change the action can write for (build and delegate members; for land, wait, bisect and route every member, dropped member and pending merge record); one foreign or unreadable source refuses the whole action with nothing written. `train land`'s gate reads the member's change (verdict, permission), landing control issue and hold dependencies, refusing before that member's writes when one is unreadable or not the selected repo's (an unresolvable hold or control issue no longer closes the train), so another repo's verdict, merge-ok or train result at the same number and SHA never counts and never closes the train; `train check` reports such a read as a blocked gate. Async land and stack intents record the `context`, `land request|resume|done`, `land stack|stack-done` and resume refuse an intent naming another repo before any read, request or drop, and `land stack --stack` takes `<owner>/<name>#<n>:<sha>` members, refusing the whole stack when one names another repo. `land-lock` records the repo's `context` and the acquiring window in `locks/merge.pid` and refuses a record naming another repo before any cleanup, signal or removal; each repo keeps its own lock. The tmux synthesizer lead brief gains a team-land paragraph. In a one-repo config a record written before this change, without `context`, stays the selected repo's; gate satisfaction, actors, fail-open paths and markers are unchanged.
